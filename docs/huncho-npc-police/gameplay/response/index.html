NPC Police

How a response plays out

The call

A crime reaches the NPC police (Calls & alerts). After Config.ResponseDelay, units spawn out of sight and drive in. The suspect gets a notice that police are on the way (Config.NotifySuspect).

On scene

Officers get out, draw and order the suspect to surrender. The suspect has Config.ComplyTime seconds to put their hands up (E, Config.SurrenderKey) and holster any gun. Officers say what's going on over their heads ("You're being stopped for Armed Robbery. Hands up!", Config.Speech).

Running or fighting

Runners are chased on foot (officers vault low fences, climb ladders and drop off ledges, Config.OfficerMovement) and tased within Config.TaserRange. Drivers get a pursuit. Shooting or ramming an officer means they shoot back.

Cuffed

An officer walks right up (Config.CloseRange) and cuffs them. Nothing is done from across the street, through a wall or from another floor.

Questions and search

Consent questions, a GSR test, the body search and a vehicle search (Search, evidence & GSR). Charges without evidence are dropped.

Transport and booking

A code 3 ride to the nearest station (the suspect can press G to skip after a while), then fingerprints, a mugshot and the holding cell (Booking).

Prison or release

Prison through your script or the built-in jail, or out on bond, a lawyer, community service or a fine (Jail, warrants & release).

Going down

  • Config.JailOnDeath = true: after the revive an officer comes to the player and arrests them. They have to actually walk there; police can't get into locked areas or houses.
  • Config.JailOnDeath = false: going down ends the response.
  • Config.DownedWarrant = true (default): a downed player who isn't arrested gets a warrant instead.

Logging out

Logging out mid-arrest (Config.JailOnDisconnect) means jail on the next login.

Players at home

Alerts that don't come from the suspect never pick a player in another instance (house routing bucket) or on another level (Config.SuspectMaxHeight, house shells).
